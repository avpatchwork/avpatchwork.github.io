The app

From a pasted order to the cables on the cart.

What it does

Built around how a show actually gets wired.

Gear

Thousands of real models

Switchers, displays, projectors, consoles, stage boxes, speakers and lights, each with its real ports.

Cables

Adapters worked out for you

Connect port to port. HDMI to SDI, XLR to TRS, long runs to fiber: the app names the cable or converter.

Lighting

DMX patch

Address fixtures by universe and channel, with runs kept to sensible lengths.

Export

Share it with the crew

PDF, spreadsheet, or a diagram image at 4K. Lengths in feet or meters.

Your work stays yours

There's no account to make. Projects stay on your device, or in your own iCloud on Apple devices. Client names and pricing on imported orders are never kept. Read the privacy policy.
