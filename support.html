Support

Questions, bugs or a missing piece of gear? Email support@avpatchwork.com. Include your device and the app version from Settings ▸ About.

New to the app? Open Settings ▸ Help ▸ How to use AV Patchwork for a one-minute walkthrough.

Common questions

How do I connect two devices?

Tap (or click on the Mac) an output dot on the right edge of a device; it turns yellow. Then tap an input dot on the left edge of another device. Tap a cable's tag to set its length or delete it.

A model I use isn't in the catalog.

Build a custom device from the Gear tab with your own inputs, outputs and power, and reuse it in any project. You can also email us the model and we'll look at adding it.

Are the power and channel numbers exact?

They come from published specs where we found them, otherwise they're estimates. Always check the manufacturer's spec sheet before planning a real show.

Is my imported order saved anywhere?

No. Orders you paste or type are read in memory to find gear and then discarded. Only the diagram you build is saved, on your device.

How do I move my projects to a new device?

Projects are stored on the device. For now, export a PDF or spreadsheet to keep a copy. Moving projects between devices is planned.

How do I change my ad and tracking choices?

Tracking: Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking on your iPhone or iPad. Ad consent (where it applies): Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ad privacy choices in the app.
