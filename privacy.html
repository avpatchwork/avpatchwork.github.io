Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

AV Patchwork (“the app”) is an independent app for AV crews. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app on iPhone, iPad or Mac.

What stays on your device

  • Projects, diagrams and custom devices are saved only on your device, inside the app. We don't receive them.
  • Settings and favorites (appearance, units, starred gear) are saved on your device. Favorites hold gear model names only.
  • Imported orders, PDFs, text and photos are read in memory to find gear and then discarded. They are never saved, uploaded or shared. Client names and pricing on them are not kept.
  • Exports (PDF, CSV, images) are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them.

We don't run servers that collect your data, and the app has no account or sign-in.

Advertising

The free iPhone and iPad app shows ads from Google AdMob. The Mac app shows no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Tracking is your choice. The app asks permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but aren't personalized with your advertising identifier. You can change this any time in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the app shows Google's consent message. You can change your choice later in the app under Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ad privacy choices.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google's use of data is covered by Google's own policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Delete a project from Projects in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
