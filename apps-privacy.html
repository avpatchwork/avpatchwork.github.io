Privacy Policy

Show Clock, DMX Pocket and Cable Labeler · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Show Clock, DMX Pocket and Cable Labeler (“the apps”) are free apps for AV and live-event crews from the makers of AV Patchwork, for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Android and Windows. This policy explains what happens to information when you use them. AV Patchwork itself has its own policy.

Your work stays on your device

  • Show Clock: your run of show, messages and timing report are saved on your device.
  • DMX Pocket: your rig and patch are saved on your device.
  • Cable Labeler: your labels are saved on your device. AV Patchwork projects and cable lists you open are read on your device to make labels; they aren't uploaded.
  • Files you share or print (CSV reports, label PDFs) are made on your device and go only where you send them.

The apps have no account or sign-in, and we don't run servers that collect your data.

Show Clock and your local network

Show Clock sends the clock to your other screens directly, without going through the internet or our servers:

  • Nearby iPhones, iPads and Macs connect over your Wi-Fi network, a direct Wi-Fi link or Bluetooth, and only after someone enters the controller's 4-digit code.
  • The browser viewer, when you switch it on, lets devices on the same network open the clock page at this device's address. The page shows only the clock (the time, segment title and speaker, and any message you send). It can't change anything, and it stops when you switch it off.

Your device may ask for permission to use the local network or Bluetooth for this.

On-device AI

Where your device supports it, the apps can use on-device AI (Apple Intelligence on iPhone, iPad and Mac; Gemini Nano on supported Android phones) to read text you paste: an agenda in Show Clock, a rig description in DMX Pocket, a cable list in Cable Labeler. This runs entirely on your device. What you type is not sent to Apple, Google, us or anyone else, and is not kept. The apps work the same without it.

Advertising (iPhone, iPad and Android)

The free iPhone, iPad and Android apps show ads from Google AdMob. The Mac and Windows apps show no ads. Ads appear only on the operator's screen, never on a stage screen or in the browser viewer.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Nothing you make in the apps (your run of show, rig or labels) is passed to Google.

Tracking is your choice. On iPhone and iPad the apps ask through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt; if you choose “Ask App Not to Track”, ads are still shown but aren't personalized with your advertising identifier. You can change this in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

Where required by law (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states), the apps show Google's consent message, and you can change your choice later under Settings ▸ Ad privacy choices in each app.

Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences. Google's use of data is covered by Google's own policy.

Tips

Tips are optional and unlock nothing. On iPhone, iPad and Mac they go through Apple's in-app purchase; on Android, through Google Play. Apple and Google handle the payment, and we don't receive your payment details.

On Windows, a tip opens a checkout page from Stripe in your browser. Stripe collects the payment details and email address needed to process the tip and send a receipt, under Stripe's privacy policy. We see the tip amount and the email address on the receipt, use them only for our records and to answer questions about the tip, and never use them for marketing.

Children

The apps are not directed at children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Deleting your data

Everything the apps save is on your device. Delete items in the app, or delete the app to remove all of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and post the new version on this page.
